## What he said (paraphrased)
1. **The 3,164 figure is a "word game":** it's one year; what matters is the cumulative loss. 中研院 researchers compared satellite images, and others compared against the city's tree inventory (樹籍), and found many trees missing.
2. **Why researchers and engineers are doing this:** the city council can't get complete data from the city. **The core issue is trust.**
3. **Fix = transparency + an SOP with residents in it:** if removals are transparent and residents took part in the meeting, nobody objects later.
4. **His 都市森林條例 (urban forest ordinance) would set:**
   - a minimum pit/root-space size before a tree can be planted;
   - removal meetings with urban designers, arborists (「樹的醫生」) and adopting residents;
   - insurers in the loop (a US lesson): coverage makes "remove or keep" disputes easier to settle.
5. **Goal: plant fairly in every area.** Cites 3-30-300: a canopy-rate target and green space within 300 m. Rules set, SOP set, **easy for the public to look up** → no distrust.
6. **Ownership is split:** mainly 公園路燈管理處, but it depends on where the tree is; other units can remove trees, and some planting involves 大地處.
