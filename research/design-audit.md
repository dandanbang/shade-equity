# Design audit — 一樹同仁 web (2026-10-13)

Independent review, refute-first. Shots in `shots/audit/` (made by `tools/audit-shots.mjs`, `tools/audit-popup.mjs`; Supabase aborted, no votes written). Bar: a Taipei voter on a phone gets it in 5 s, and the 2-minute projector demo lands on one number.

## P0 — hurts the demo or the vote

1. **Phone: the map isn't visible.** At rest the hero takes 0–390 px of an 844 px screen (`m-home.png`). After 種回 it grows to 530 px (`m-planted.png`), so most of the planted dots sit under the card. Opening a 里 puts the panel at 372–836 px *on top of* the hero (measured overlap 158 px; `m-li.png`, `m-tapli.png`). What's left is a 0 px map.
   **Why:** phones are where the vote is decided, and the product is the map.
   **Fix:** under 720 px, collapse the hero to eyebrow + h1 + button (~170 px) and move lede, seg and `#mode-desc` behind a 「說明」 toggle. After planting, swap the hero body for a one-line result strip (`需要優先種：46% 種在最需要的里 · 3 倍`). While `#panel` is open, add `body.sheet-open #hero{display:none}`. Make `#panel` a real bottom sheet: grab handle, `max-height:60dvh`, `border-radius:16px 16px 0 0`, flush to `bottom:0`.

2. **Phone: `fitBounds` fails, so the selected 里 is never framed.** Padding is hard-coded `left:420,right:400` (app.js `select`). On a 390 px screen MapLibre logs "Map cannot fit within canvas" and does nothing.
   **Fix:** compute padding from the layout: `const m = innerWidth<720; padding = m ? {top:16,left:16,right:16,bottom: panel.offsetHeight+16} : {top:60,bottom:60,left:hero.offsetWidth+40,right:panel.offsetWidth+40}`. Run it after the panel is unhidden.

3. **No share preview: shared links show up blank on LINE/FB.** `index.html` has 0 `og:` tags and no `og:image` (checked live with curl). In a public vote, links get passed around LINE groups, and a blank preview gets skipped.
   **Fix:** add `og:title` 「去年台北移除了 3,164 棵樹，該種回哪裡？」, `og:description`, an `og:image` (1200×630 PNG: the planted-dots map plus the "3 倍" line), `og:url`, `twitter:card=summary_large_image`, and `apple-touch-icon`. Do the same for `about.html`. Also add a 「分享這個里」 button in the panel that calls `navigator.share({url: location.origin+location.pathname+'?plant=1&li='+code})` and falls back to copying the link.

4. **The demo's payoff number isn't on screen.** The about page says it: 「同樣的樹，需要優先種讓 3 倍的樹落在最需要的地方」. The map shows three equal-weight stat tiles (`d-plant.png`), and two of them need decoding: `+8.94°C` 「種下處平均地表高溫」 is an anomaly with no reference point, and `680k` is jargon where Taiwanese readers expect 萬. Each tile also prints the comparison value *above* its own label, so it reads 46.4% → 平均種：15.6% → label.
   **Fix:** above the tiles, a 28 px headline: **「3 倍」的樹種到最需要的 20% 里** (46% vs 16%). In each tile, put the label first, then the big number, then `平均種 16%`. Rewrite the copy: 「比全市平均熱 +8.9°C」 (1 decimal), 「所得中位數 68 萬」. Write lower-is-better with an arrow or words (「所得較低 ↓」).

5. **Green means two opposite things.** The fill ramp ends in green for 「已達標」, and planting sites are green dots. On green 里 (Beitou, Wenshan hills) the planted dots disappear (`d-plant.png`). The hero also paints *bad* news green (「只有 4 個真的空著」, 「15,534」), and the legend is hidden entirely on phones (`#legend{display:none}`), so a voter gets no key at all.
   **Fix:** reserve green for trees only. Make the ramp single-hue need: `#b8380c → #f08c3a → #f6d88f → #f3efe6` (cream = 已達標), and use the same stops in `p-tes` colour. Put the hero numbers in `--ink` bold, with `--sun` for loss. On mobile, show a 1-line legend chip under the hero (`▬ 橘＝最缺樹蔭　● 種樹點`).

6. **Projector 1280×720: cards collide and Taipei sits off-centre.** In the planted state the legend overlaps the compare tiles (`p-li.png`, legend top 560 < hero bottom 590). The footer runs under the panel. At rest the neediest west side (萬華/大同) is half under the hero (`p-home.png`).
   **Fix:** on load, `map.fitBounds(TAIPEI_BBOX,{padding:{left:hero.offsetWidth+32,top:24,bottom:24,right:24}})` in place of the fixed `center/zoom`. Hide the legend when `#compare` is visible and `innerHeight<800` (or move the legend into the hero). Hide the footer below 1366 px. Add `?demo=1` → `html{font-size:18px}` and `.small{font-size:14px}`: 12 px text can't be read from the back of a room.

## P1

7. **Tapping a dot also opens the 里 panel, which covers the popup** (`m-popup.png`: panel open = true, popup = true; desktop too). **Fix:** in the `li-fill` click handler, `if (map.queryRenderedFeatures(e.point,{layers:['pits-plan','spots','pits-empty']}).length) return;`. Also give popups to the ring and gray dots. Today only planned dots respond.
8. **The ask is buried in the panel.** Order is score → 4 factors → 8-row facts table → 建議種樹點 → howto → list. On a phone the first verify button is about 3 screens down (`m-li-scrolled.png`). **Fix:** put 建議種樹點 (count + the first 3 sites + 「幫忙確認」) directly after the factors. Fold the facts table into `<details><summary>更多數字</summary>`. Cut the howto to one line: 「看街景 → 回來按一個 · 30 秒」.
9. **Verify buttons are too small and too quiet.** They measure 100×26 px on a phone (measured), the selected state is a faint `#e3f0e7` tint (`d-verify.png`), there's no thank-you, failures are swallowed by `.catch(()=>{})`, and every tap POSTs another row. **Fix:** `.verify button{min-height:44px;font-size:14px}`. Make `.on` solid `--green` with white text, and put `✓` in the label. After a tap, show an inline 「謝謝！已記錄 · 你是第 N 位」 and bump the tally optimistically. Disable re-sending the same verdict and debounce changes. If the POST fails, show 「沒送出，稍後再試」. Add `aria-pressed`.
10. **「看街景 ↗」 breaks mid-word** (「看街 / 景 ↗」, `d-popup.png`) and is styled as body text, though it's step 1 of the task. **Fix:** make it a full-width outline button at the top of each item (`min-height:44px; white-space:nowrap`), with the meta line (周邊樹蔭 0% · 離人潮 12 公尺) under it.
11. **Voters can't find their own 里.** The only way in is to pan and tap a polygon. Wanting to see "my 里" is the strongest reason a voter would share. **Fix:** add 「找我的里」: a `GeolocateControl`, plus a search input that filters the 456 `TOWNNAME+VILLNAME` strings and calls `select()`.
12. **Two controls do the same job.** After planting, both the seg and the big button switch modes. Before planting, the seg asks the user to choose a method before they've seen anything. **Fix:** hide `.seg` and `#mode-desc` until the first plant. After that, the big button is the only toggle and the seg becomes a read-only label of the current mode, or drop the seg entirely.
13. **First 5 seconds: three messages, no instruction.** The lede mixes the data bug, a call to fix data, and the planting goal. Nothing says "tap a 里". **Fix:** under the h1, one line: 「按下面的按鈕，看這些樹該種回哪裡；點任何一個里，看它為什麼需要樹蔭。」 Move the 4-of-30 data-bug line into the panel howto and the about page. Change the link to 「方法與資料 →」.
14. **No loading state, and the button works before the data loads.** The map is blank for 1–2 s and the hero shows `—` (`m-loading.png`). Pressing 種回 early throws, because `setFilter` runs on a layer that doesn't exist yet and `summary` is still null. **Fix:** set `#btn-plant` disabled with the label 「載入地圖中…」 and enable it in the `Promise.all` `.then`. Use `--` instead of `—` inside a shimmer.
15. **A 里 that already has full shade still asks 「為什麼這裡需要樹蔭？」 and shows 「第 364 / 456 里」** (93 里 tie at 100; `m-tapli.png`). **Fix:** when TES ≥ 99, use the heading 「這裡的樹蔭已達標 🌳」, the verdict 「全台北 93 個里已達標」, no rank, and collapse the factors under 「其他指標」.
16. **A 里 with no sites still shows the how-to box, a numbered 「1. 此里沒有分配到…」, and an active CSV button** (`d-nosite-li.png`, 南港 63000090008). **Fix:** render the empty state as a `<p>`, not an `<li>`. Hide `.howto` and `#p-csv` when `list.length===0`. Add the next action: 「看附近需要的里 →」 (select the nearest 里 with sites).
17. **Terms drift.** The same things go by 疑似空樹穴 / 空樹穴 / 其他疑似空樹穴, 新增樹穴 / 可新增樹穴, 種樹點 / 建議種樹點 / 建議優先確認／種樹, and 人潮 / 人流. **Fix:** lock three terms everywhere (legend, list, popup, CSV, about): **種樹點** (umbrella), **空樹穴（待確認）**, **新增樹穴（需現勘）**. Standardise on 人潮. Use 公尺, not `m`, in user-facing copy.
18. **Units read like English.** Panel facts show `514k` and tiles show `680k`. **Fix:** use `wan()` everywhere (「51 萬」) and 1 decimal for °C. The panel fact 「樹冠覆蓋（3 m 以上，目標 30%）」 becomes 「樹冠覆蓋（目標 30%）」, with the 3 m detail moved to about.

## P2

19. **Contrast:** chip `.lo` is 2.85:1 and `.mid` is 3.3:1 at 11.5 px, both below AA 4.5:1. The gray dot on Positron is 2.9:1. **Fix:** `.chip.lo{color:#5f6662}` and `.chip.mid{color:#8a5a0f}`. Change the gray dot to `#6b716e`, radius +1, white stroke. (Light touch only: factor rows are approved, and 「中」 → 「中需求」 would make the three chips parallel.)
20. **Keyboard and screen readers:** the `role="tab"` buttons have no `aria-selected`/tabpanel. There's no `:focus-visible` style (`d-focus.png` shows the default blue ring). `#close` is 25×28 px. The map can't be reached by keyboard. **Fix:** `aria-pressed` on the seg, `:focus-visible{outline:3px solid #2f7d4f;outline-offset:2px}`, `#close{width:44px;height:44px}`, Esc closes the panel, and the 里 search (#11) serves as the keyboard path.
21. **Desktop h1 orphans 「樹。」** onto its own line at 380 px (`d-home.png`). **Fix:** wrap 「3,164 棵樹」 in `<span style="white-space:nowrap">`, or `text-wrap:balance`.
22. **Zoom ≥15 loses the need colour.** Fill opacity drops to 0.18 (`d-z16.png`), so at street level you can't tell which 里 is needy. **Fix:** hold opacity at 0.3 until z16, and thicken the 里 boundary line (0.6 → 1.2, `#fff`) so streets still read.
23. **Score colour thresholds don't match the ramp:** `p-tes` uses 45/70, the ramp uses 20/45/65/80. **Fix:** derive the `p-tes` colour from the same interpolate stops (one `rampColor(TES)` helper).
24. **Data weight is fine but easy to trim:** about 580 KB gzipped (CSV 392 KB + GeoJSON 188 KB, live). GeoJSON coordinates carry 15 decimals. **Fix:** round to 6 decimals in the builder (about −50%), and drop the unused properties (`E,gap,n_*`). `.tally` is defined twice in `style.css` with conflicting colours; delete the first.
25. **About page on a phone:** the 2-column TOC collides (「5. 需要優先種 vs 平均種10.」, `m-about.png`), and voters hit methodology before a reason to care. **Fix:** `@media(max-width:600px){nav.toc ol{columns:1}}`. Open with a 3-line 「一句話」 box (problem · what we do · how you help) and move 版本紀錄 to GitHub.
