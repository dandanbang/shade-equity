# 台北行道樹怎麼種下去：真實流程（2026-10-13）
Goal: a hand-off map that does not overclaim. Every fact carries a URL. **[inf]** = our inference. **[2nd]** = press or secondary source. **[gap]** = searched and not found. Tender data comes from the PCC mirror `pcc-api.openfun.app`, which copies web.pcc.gov.tw; to check a tender, open its job number on web.pcc.gov.tw.

## 1. Step by step
**0. Who owns it.** 公園處 (工務局公園路燈工程管理處) numbers, records and patrols every street tree, and handles dead or damaged ones (行道樹管理維護自治條例 §4). No public tree may be removed or moved without its consent (§7). https://laws.gov.taipei/Law/LawSearch/LawArticleContent/FL004003

**1. What starts a planting.** There are four real triggers:
- **(a) Replacement after removal or storm damage.** This is the main flow. In 2025, 3,164 trees were removed or lost to disasters; 公園處 says 2,630 of them (>80%) were dead, decayed, diseased or storm-hit. https://newtalk.tw/news/view/2026-07-12/1046702 In the same year 公園處 replanted only **778 street trees (24.6%)**. https://news.ltn.com.tw/news/Taipei/breakingnews/5503570 · https://news.ltn.com.tw/news/Taipei/breakingnews/5505002
- **(b) A 里長 asks at 「市長與里長有約」.** The mayor issues an order, and the item is tracked (列管) for 1–3 months. Example: at 松山區 on 2025-04-25, 龍田里 and 東榮里 reported that trees knocked down by Typhoon 康芮 (Oct 2024) had **still not been replanted about 6 months later**. The order: 公園處 replants within 1 month, and **新工處 fills in pits within 10 m of an intersection within 1 month** (minutes 11408). https://www-ws.gov.taipei/001/Upload/334/relfile/15936/136924/9070b56a-0cf6-4f1c-9a9c-0e23d20fe23a.pdf
- **(c) A 新工處 sidewalk project.** 公園處 asks 新工處 for pits of at least **1.2 m square**, in continuous strips where possible. https://news.tvbs.com.tw/life/996375 New pits get built when a sidewalk is rebuilt **[inf]**.
- **(d) A resident files through 「臺北市陳情系統1999」 (renamed 2023-10-01).** https://dweb.cjcu.edu.tw/ead/news/39046 The 1999 「路樹處理」 dispatch item covers hazards (48 h), not planting (see implementation.md §2). A planting request becomes an ordinary 陳情 sent to 公園處. Agencies must reply within **6 working days**, or within 30 calendar days with an interim reply if the case is complex (文書處理實施要點 §87) **[2nd: read in a school's copy, not checked on laws.gov.taipei]**. A reply is not a planting.
- **[gap]** We found no published annual 補植 plan and no list of 補植 priorities. A councilor is demanding exactly that: "先把行道樹補植計畫說清楚". https://news.ltn.com.tw/news/Taipei/breakingnews/5505002

**2. Site survey (現勘): what gets checked.**
- **Pit size:** ≥1.2 m square is 公園處's own ask (TVBS above). **[gap]** We found no codified 公園處 pit standard.
- **Sidewalk clear width:** The national 市區道路及附屬工程設計規範 recommends ≥2.5 m and requires ≥1.5 m in general (≥1.2 m on roads ≤12 m wide). Taipei's rule for sidewalk protrusions sets no facility where clear width is <1.5 m. https://myway.nlma.gov.tw/upload/rule/1646877660N2vjD.pdf **[2nd: from a search summary]** One expert says Taipei bars trees on sidewalks only 2.5 m wide. https://udn.com/news/story/7323/8959708 **[2nd, not verified in a regulation]**
- **Sight distance and intersections:** pits within **10 m of an intersection** were filled in, not replanted (松山 minutes above). We treat 10 m as a practical rule. **[inf: it is one mayoral order, not a codified standard]**
- **Utilities:** a public utility may go into a pit only with 公園處 permission and at a distance 公園處 announces (§6). **[gap]** We could not find that distance. Pits may not be closed or shrunk without permission (§9).
- **Road-dig approvals:** 新工處 runs 道路挖掘管理自治條例. Pipes under sidewalks must be ≥50 cm deep, and there is a **3-year no-dig rule after new paving**. https://laws.gov.taipei/law/LawSearch/LawExport/FL003974?type=0 Planting an existing pit probably needs no dig permit, but opening a new pit in fresh paving runs into the 3-year rule **[inf]**.

**3. Budget and procurement.** All of this work runs on **開口契約 (預約工程)**: the contract is awarded on a total price, signed at unit prices, and paid per work order (施工單) until 31 Dec or until the ceiling is used up. There is **no planting-only tender**: planting sits inside regional greening contracts **[inf: the titles say 花木綠化; the scope documents were not read]**.

- **Cost per tree: [gap].** §10 says 公園處 sets unit prices for the tree, planting, aftercare and removal. We found no published price schedule (the penalty table cites only "基本單價"). https://www.laws.taipei.gov.tw/law/File/0000296924 One opinion piece puts a 10 cm tree at about NT$10,000, inflated by naming species in tenders. https://www.twreporter.org/a/opinion-street-trees **[2nd/opinion]**
- **Timing [inf]:** tenders are issued Nov–Jan and awarded Jan–Mar, so a request made after the budget is set can only draw on the current contract's remaining ceiling. Anything bigger waits for next year's budget, which the council adopts around December.

**4. Season, aftercare, survival.**
- **Season:** The Taipei transplant rule (樹木移植作業規範, 2022) puts deciduous species at Nov–Mar; evergreens and palms have their own windows. https://laws.gov.taipei/law/LawSearch/LawPrint/FL076275?type=0
- **Aftercare (保活):** The transplant rule requires ≥**1 year** from acceptance, with the contractor doing the watering, weeding, pruning and **補植**. Survival is judged against bark-damage and canopy thresholds (≥80% of the form at planting). For new street-tree plantings, **1 year** is also the compensation unit in §10. **[gap]** We did not read the greening contract's own 保固 clause. After year 1 the tree reverts to 公園處 routine care, and opinion pieces describe that as the point where "no one is responsible". https://www.twreporter.org/a/opinion-street-trees **[opinion]**
- **Survival tracking:** 113S026 pays a consultant to survey 新補植 trees. **[gap]** No survival rate has been published.

**5. Citizen and 里長 routes to a tree.**
- **陳情 / 1999:** a reply within 6 working days (or 30 days for complex cases). It is a reply, not a commitment.
- **市長與里長有約:** the strongest lever, because it produces an order with a deadline and is tracked for 1–3 months. The 松山 case shows it turning a 6-month stall into a 1-month order.
- **議員:** questions in council and budget pressure (洪婉臻 2026 on the 778 vs 3,164 gap).
- **認養:** a 3-year term to care for an existing tree. It does not cover planting. https://laws.gov.taipei/law/LawSearch/LawPrint/FL002833?type=0

## 2. Bottlenecks (where requests die)
1. **Removal outpaces replanting:** 3,164 removed vs 778 replanted (2025). 公園處 counted 13,225 "new trees" by adding in 大地處 hillside forest (4,842) and 都發局 plantings. https://news.ltn.com.tw/news/Taipei/breakingnews/5505002
2. **Storm backlog:** 康芮 knocked down 2,800 trees (newtalk above). Six months later, 松山 里長 were still asking for replanting.
3. **Sites lost for good:** pits near intersections get paved over (10 m order). Narrow sidewalks fail the 1.5/2.5 m tests. Some sidewalk widenings remove trees: the setback for a lane on 松智路 moved trees at City Hall. https://udn.com/news/story/7323/9602464
4. **Budget mix:** pruning (NT$210M) is twice greening (NT$103M), and greening also pays for flowers and shrubs **[inf]**. Planting competes with everything else in the same contract.
5. **Utilities:** the distance under §6 is unpublished, and 新工處 says relocating pipes makes sidewalk widening complex. https://news.tvbs.com.tw/english/2818117 **[2nd]**
6. **Shop owners and residents:** **[gap for Taipei]**. Shops opposing sidewalk works is documented in 台中 (沙鹿). https://news.pts.org.tw/article/732463 In 民生社區, a 里長 criticized residents who skipped a year of consultations and only objected at construction. https://news.pts.org.tw/article/816822 **[2nd]**
7. **Audit:** **[gap]** We found no 審計處 report on Taipei street-tree planting; 新北's 2025 audit is the closest. https://udn.com/news/story/7323/8960257

## 3. Community participation precedents (Taipei)
- **參與式預算 (2015–):** by late 2016, 63 of 167 proposals had been adopted; there was no budget cap. Execution was then sorted green/yellow/red by feasibility, and some proposers heard nothing for months. https://www.twreporter.org/a/participatory-budget-challenge **[gap]** We found no completed tree-planting PB project to cite.
- **田園城市 (2015–16):** 92,179 m² of community gardens and 25,674 person-visits of planting; 產業局 gave up to NT$200k per community. https://newtalk.tw/news/view/2016-11-21/79274 **[2nd]** The successor, 產業局's 「公有閒置空間綠化工程單價發包案」, is still tendered (115 北區 awarded at NT$7.37M, 1150401C0037).
- **認養 (3-year terms):** the rules exist. **[gap]** No adoption counts are published.
- **民生社區 富民生態公園:** a year of needs surveys and design briefings, then 6 replacement 流蘇 trees. https://news.pts.org.tw/article/816822 **[2nd]**
- **Campaign promises (2026-08-26):** 沈伯洋 proposes 3-30-300, an urban-forest fund of NT$1B a year (about NT$500M spent each year), a cross-agency 都市森林委員會 and a citizen-participation mechanism. He also blames lowest-bid procurement, close-out deadlines and agencies passing the buck. This is a pledge, not a program. https://newtalk.tw/news/view/2026-08-26/1055724 **[2nd]** At the national level, 環境部 plans an urban-forest legal framework (「1人1樹、1社區1綠蔭」) by year-end. https://udn.com/news/story/7266/9640018 **[2nd]**

## 4. What our tool should claim and not claim
**Realistic hand-off chain [inf, assembled from the sources above]:**
里民 confirm empty pits → **里長 files at 「市長與里長有約」 or with 公園處** → 公園處 現勘 (pit ≥1.2 m, clear width, 10 m from intersection, utilities) → if it passes, a work order on that year's **花木綠化開口契約** → planted in season (Nov–Mar works best) → **~1 year contractor 保活** → 公園處 routine care, optionally with 認養.
- **Timeline [inf]:** if the contract has room left, about **1–6 months** from a tracked mayoral order (the 1-month order is the best case). If it needs new budget or a new pit, plan for **the next fiscal year (12–18 months)**. A new pit on a 新工處 sidewalk follows that project's schedule and the 3-year no-dig rule.

**Claim:**
- "Here are N candidate sites that pass the screens we can check (pit, width, intersection distance), ready for 公園處 現勘."
- "Here is a 里長 proposal in the 「市長與里長有約」 format, naming 公園處 (planting) and 新工處 (pits) as the responsible agencies."
- "Track promised → planted → alive after year 1 (保活 ends) → years 2–3."

**Do not claim:**
- That a proposal or 1999 ticket **causes** planting. 公園處 decides after 現勘, and the budget is set in advance.
- That a site is **plantable**. We cannot see utilities, the §6 distance or pending 新工處 works. Call it "a candidate for 現勘".
- A cost per tree. No official unit price is public; if we show a number, label it an estimate.
- That adoption plants trees. 認養 cares for trees that already exist.
- That planting = survival. 保活 lasts about 1 year, and no survival rate is published.
